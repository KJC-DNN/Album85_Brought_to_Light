## Long Is the Way, the River, and the Iron Choir

Song 3 of Brought to Light. Intercession on SCP 4101–4200: official 4113 Long Is the Way You Must Wander, 4105 They Came From the River, 4140 Sarkic Containment Procedures, 4101 The World’s Greatest Teacher is History, and kin. Remnant Symphony (Road / River / Bell / Throne) is the sung close of this song. No ritual. Not affiliated with the SCP Wiki. KJC-DNN / Remnant Christian Worship.
