# Album 85 — Brought to Light
KJC-DNN / Remnant Christian Worship

Album 85 — Brought to Light (85.0.0) is a seven-song intercession cycle under Jesus Christ as Lord of the seas, of history, and of what was hidden. Official SCP Series 5 titles from 4001–4600 are named beside prayer overlays only. The spine is 4005 (veiled market / lamp that is not the Light), 4001 Alexandria Eternal, 4100 Future Imperfect, 4113 Long Is the Way, 4140 Sarkic cave-song refused as worship, 4300 To Lose One’s Soul, 4311 false care, 4419 / 4444 choir and empty throne as files, 4519 / 4520 verdant abyss and ashen veil.
Each song uses Core 7 + Expanded 7 + New 7 + Modern 6 (27 forms). Remnant Symphony is the sung close on Songs 3–7. Song 4 also has a spoken Short Story and Short Story Song (Jesus keeps the Book). Audio from Song 4 onward is MP3. One Story & Prayer MP4 per song. Holy Jest does not mock the Trinity. No ritual instruction. Not affiliated with the SCP Wiki. KJC-DNN / Remnant Christian Worship.
